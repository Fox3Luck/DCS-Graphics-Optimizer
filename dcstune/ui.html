Help make this tool better? (optional)

May the tool send the result of each test to Fox3, the makers of this tool? It is anonymous.

  • What is sent: your processor, graphics card and driver, memory, monitor resolution, DCS version, VR headset model and VR software if you have one, your DCS graphics and VR settings, the measured frame rates, and whether you applied or undid the recommendation.
  • What is not sent: your name, Windows or DCS account, pilot name, folder names, serial numbers, screenshots or controls. Each test only gets a random number; there is no number for you or your PC. Results are stored without your internet address.
  • What it is for: the tool estimates what each setting costs on your hardware. Real results from many different PCs and headsets make those estimates and the recommendations better for everyone.
  • After every test the same information is saved as audit.json in the report folder, so you can read exactly what was sent. You can add a comment to a result if you want to tell us more.
  • If you say no, nothing is ever sent and the tool works exactly the same. You can change your answer at any time at the bottom of this window.

What do the tests do, and how long do they take?
  • Each test starts DCS by itself several times and closes it again. Close other programs that use the graphics card first (games, video renders, AI tools); the test refuses to start if the card is busy.
  • Each time, the computer flies three short scenes on the Caucasus map (a city pass, an airfield ramp, a cloud layer) while every frame is timed.
  • Test and optimize: 2 to 5 starts, usually 10 to 20 minutes. Measures your settings, then tests and corrects a recommendation.
  • Thorough test: about 10 starts, usually 35 to 60 minutes. Also measures what each expensive setting costs on your PC; most of them are measured inside one long DCS session that restarts only the mission.
  • The goal is your monitor's refresh rate (up to 120 FPS) in the worst moments; on slower PCs it steps down to 90, 72 or 60.
  • Resolution, vertical sync, VR, controls and sound are never changed. Your settings file is restored after every start.
  • Nothing is sent over the internet. The full guide is in the project's User Guide.
