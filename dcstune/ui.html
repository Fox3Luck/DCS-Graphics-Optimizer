The test starts DCS several times by itself and closes it again. Leave the PC alone while it runs. Your settings are put back after every test; nothing is changed until you press Apply.

What do the tests do, and how long do they take?
  • Each test starts DCS by itself several times and closes it again. Close other programs that use the graphics card first (games, video renders, AI tools); the test refuses to start if the card is busy.
  • Each time, the computer flies three short scenes on the Caucasus map (a city pass, an airfield ramp, a cloud layer) while every frame is timed.
  • Test and optimize: 2 to 5 starts, usually 10 to 20 minutes. Measures your settings, then tests and corrects a recommendation.
  • Thorough test: about 10 starts, usually 35 to 60 minutes. Also measures what each expensive setting costs on your PC; most of them are measured inside one long DCS session that restarts only the mission.
  • The goal is your monitor's refresh rate (up to 120 FPS) in the worst moments; on slower PCs it steps down to 90, 72 or 60.
  • Resolution, vertical sync, VR, controls and sound are never changed. Your settings file is restored after every start.
  • Nothing is sent over the internet. The full guide is in the project's User Guide.
